Key Characteristics:  
1. High purchase amount (5.75) almost entirely from installments (5.74 vs 0.17 for one-off) 
2. Highest installment purchase frequency (0.67) 
3. Very low one-off purchase frequency (0.006)

Targeted Schemes:  
1. 0% interest on installment plans for longer periods 
2. Early payment discounts for installment plans 
3. Special offers at stores with installment payment options 
4. Extended warranties on products purchased with installments 
5. Milestone rewards for completing installment payment cycles 
6. Upg6rade offers for high-value installment purchases

Strategic Recommendations:
For Cluster 0: Focus on converting cash advance users to more profitable purchase behaviors through incentives and education. 
For Cluster 1: Retain these high-value customers through enhanced loyalty programs and exclusive benefits. 
For Cluster 2: Encourage higher spending on one-off purchases and introduce benefits of installment options. 
For Cluster 3: Manage credit risk while maximizing revenue from this high-activity segment through tailored offers. 
For Cluster 4: Expand their usage into other product categories while maintaining their preference for installment purchases.
